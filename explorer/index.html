What applies to my system?

Tick the capabilities your system has and say how it uses them. ARC lists the risks that apply and the controls they carry. Anything you leave unstated is taken in its most cautious form.

In every system: LLM, Instructions, Tools, Agentic Architecture, Roles and Access Controls, Monitoring and Traceability.

Counts are before any waiver. A Level 0 control is waived only when the capability or component it concerns is absent from your system: mark those below, and record the evidence in your assessment. Scoring never waives a Level 0 control. Unstated context counts as the most cautious case: each capability at the highest authority it allows, with effects that cannot be undone and that reach outside the organisation (see how a capability is used).
