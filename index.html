ARC in brief

A system's elements give rise to risks, and each risk comes with controls. Pick out the capabilities your system has, and the register gives you the risks that apply and what to do about them.

1 · Elements

What the system is made of and what it can do.

Components
Design
Capabilities

20 elements. Every system has the design and the other components; Memory and Protocols only where present, and capabilities vary.

→give rise to
2 · Risks

What can go wrong because of those elements.

Failure modes
How it goes wrong: an agent errs or misbehaves, is manipulated or misused, or a tool or safeguard fails.
Hazards
The safety or security harm that results.
66 risks in register 2.0.0-draft.1, each tied to the elements, or the combination of capabilities, it arises from.
→which require
3 · Controls

What to put in place: 103 controls, at three levels.

Level 0
Adopt as is wherever the risk applies.
Level 1
Adopt or adapt.
Level 2
Consider, especially for high-risk systems.
Residual risk
What the controls don't fully address.
