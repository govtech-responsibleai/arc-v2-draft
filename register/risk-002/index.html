RISK-002 · Insufficient alignment of LLM behaviour

RISK-002Agent MisbehaviourSafety and Security
Arises from LLM

Every agentic system has this element, so this risk applies to every system.

This risk arises when an LLM's learned objectives and behaviors do not reliably align with intended user goals, system instructions, or organizational policies, leading to inappropriate, unsafe, or undesired outputs. Misalignment may surface as failure to follow constraints, inconsistent reasoning, or behavior that diverges from expected norms in edge cases or complex scenarios.

Hazards: Application (HZ-02), Inappropriate content (HZ-07), Compromised user safety (HZ-08)

Controls

Section titled “Controls”
Level 0CTRL-0007Log all LLM inputs and outputs for regular review Level 0CTRL-0038Conduct safety verification with domain-relevant stress tests and adversarial scenarios before deployment and on a regular cadence afterwards Level 1CTRL-0005Conduct structured evaluation of multiple LLMs for instruction-following, performance, and safety before deployment Level 1CTRL-0008Implement automated alerts when agent behaviour drifts from predefined thresholds Level 1CTRL-0042Require explicit human confirmation before executing high-impact or irreversible tool actionsStricter in some systems Level 2CTRL-0004Review the LLM's system card to inform risk assessment and model selection

Level 0 controls are adopted as is wherever this risk applies; one is waived only when the capability or component it concerns is absent. See control levels.
