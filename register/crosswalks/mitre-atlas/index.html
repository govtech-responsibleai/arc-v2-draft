Crosswalk: MITRE ATLAS

ARC maps its risks to MITRE ATLAS (Adversarial Threat Landscape for AI Systems) (2026.10 (28 October 2026)). Each mapping points to the nearest corresponding entry; it is not a claim that the two are the same.

Read it from either side: below, each item in that framework lists the ARC entries that map to it, and each ARC entry's own page lists its crosswalks.
