# RISK-031 · Inaccurate promises or statements in official communications

ARC risk register 2.0.0-draft.1. https://govtech-responsibleai.github.io/arc-v2-draft/register/risk-031/

This risk arises when an agent makes commitments, assurances, or public statements that are incorrect, unsupported, or exceed the organisation’s actual intentions or capabilities. As a result, the organisation may face reputational damage, legal exposure, or loss of public trust due to unmet expectations or misinformation.

- Arises from: Official Communication (CAP-05)
- Failure mode: Agent Error
- Type: Safety
- Hazards: Misrepresentation (HZ-09), Financial or contractual loss (HZ-10)

## Controls

- CTRL-0053 (Level 0): Require human approval for communications on sensitive matters
- CTRL-0094 (Level 0): Measure the quality of human oversight and redesign approval steps that have become rubber stamps
- CTRL-0054 (Level 1): Limit agent communications to standard processes with predefined templates
- CTRL-0055 (Level 1): Provide alternative channels for users to clarify communications or provide feedback
