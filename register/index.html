Draft for review. ARC 2.0, draft 1 (23 November 2026). This is not the published framework, which is version 1.3.0. How to comment

The risk register

Register 2.0.0-draft.1, released 2026-11-23: 66 risks, each tied to the elements or the combination of capabilities it arises from, and 103 controls (46 at Level 0). Every risk, control and element has its own page and address, so you can link to one entry or cite it.

  • All risks, with filters: filter by element, failure mode, hazard or type, or search
  • All controls, by level
  • All elements: the components, design elements and capabilities that risks arise from
  • Capability combinations: the rules for capabilities that are dangerous together
  • What applies to my system?: pick capabilities and how they are used, and see the risks and Level 0 controls that apply
  • Register data: the whole register as JSON and CSV, with a JSON Schema
  • Full text: every entry on one page
  • Crosswalks to the OWASP Top 10 for Agentic Applications, OWASP agentic threats, CSA Securing Agentic AI addendum, IMDA Model AI Governance Framework for Agentic AI, MITRE ATLAS and Five Eyes guidance on agentic AI, and the changelog

To cite an entry, give the register version and its ID, for example "ARC risk register 2.0.0-draft.1, RISK-034".

3 retired entries keep their pages, so that links to them still work; each points to the entry that replaces it.
