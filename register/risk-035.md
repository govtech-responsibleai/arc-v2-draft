# RISK-035 · Unreliable information or websites

ARC risk register 2.0.0-draft.1. https://govtech-responsibleai.github.io/arc-v2-draft/register/risk-035/

This risk arises when an agent retrieves and presents information from websites that are inaccurate, outdated, biased, or otherwise unreliable. As a result, users may be misinformed or make incorrect decisions based on content that has not been adequately validated or corroborated.

- Arises from: Internet and Search Access (CAP-07)
- Failure mode: Tool or Resource Malfunction
- Type: Safety
- Hazards: Compromised user safety (HZ-08), Misrepresentation (HZ-09)

## Controls

- CTRL-0063 (Level 1): Prioritise search results from verified, high-quality domains
