# RISK-029 · Generation of copyrighted content

ARC risk register 2.0.0-draft.1. https://govtech-responsibleai.github.io/arc-v2-draft/register/risk-029/

This risk arises when an agent generates content that reproduces or closely resembles copyrighted material without appropriate rights or attribution. As a result, the system may infringe intellectual property laws, expose the organisation to legal liability, or violate licensing and usage terms.

- Arises from: Multimodal Understanding and Generation (CAP-04)
- Failure mode: Agent Error
- Type: Safety
- Hazards: Illegal and CBRNE activities (HZ-05)

## Controls

- CTRL-0051 (Level 1): Implement input guardrails to detect and decline requests to generate copyrighted content
