# Agentic Risk & Capability Framework (ARC)

> ARC, the Agentic Risk & Capability Framework, works out an agentic AI system's safety and security risks from what it is made of and what it can do, and recommends technical controls for each.

Register 2.0.0-draft.1, released 2026-11-23: 66 risks and 103 controls (46 at Level 0).

Every page on this site has a Markdown copy at the same address with `.md` (for example https://govtech-responsibleai.github.io/arc-v2-draft/arc_framework/in-five-minutes.md), and [llms-full.txt](https://govtech-responsibleai.github.io/arc-v2-draft/llms-full.txt) has them all in one file.
