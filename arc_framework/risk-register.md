# Interactive Risk Register

Here is our baseline **Risk Register** which we developed for the ARC Framework. It identifies a total of 66 risks arising from the different elements of agentic AI systems that we identified earlier, and recommends 103 controls to mitigate these risks. This is register 2.0.0-draft.1; see the [changelog](https://govtech-responsibleai.github.io/arc-v2-draft/arc_framework/changelog/) for what changed in each version and how to cite a specific one. The same content is also available as [plain text](https://govtech-responsibleai.github.io/arc-v2-draft/arc_framework/register-full-text/), with a link for every risk and control.

> **How to use the Risk Register**
>
> Select a risk to open its page: its description, hazards, controls by level, crosswalks to other frameworks, and sources. Filter the list by element, failure mode, hazard or type, or search by words or an ID. A risk whose elements are joined with "+" arises from a combination, and applies only to systems that have all of them. The page keeps your filters in its address, so you can share a filtered list.
