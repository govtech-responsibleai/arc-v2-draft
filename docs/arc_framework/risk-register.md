# Interactive Risk Register

Here is our baseline **Risk Register** which we developed for the ARC Framework. It identifies a total of <span data-arc="risks">66</span> risks arising from the different elements of agentic AI systems that we identified earlier, and recommends <span data-arc="controls">103</span> controls to mitigate these risks. This is register <span data-arc="version">2.0.0</span>; see the [changelog](changelog.md) for what changed in each version and how to cite a specific one. The same content is also available as [plain text](register-full-text.md), with a link for every risk and control.

!!! note "How to use the Risk Register"

    Select a risk to open its page: its description, hazards, controls by level, crosswalks to other frameworks, and sources. Filter the list by element, failure mode, hazard or type, or search by words or an ID. A risk whose elements are joined with "+" arises from a combination, and applies only to systems that have all of them. The page keeps your filters in its address, so you can share a filtered list.
